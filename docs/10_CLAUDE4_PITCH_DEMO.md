## Input

## Produce

## Demo Sequence
1. Introduce farmer problem.
2. Enter farm.
3. Select crop and sowing date.
4. Show Digital Farm Twin.
5. Show zones.
6. Open high water-stress zone.
7. Show evidence.
8. Explain why.
9. Show confidence/data freshness.
10. Show recommendation.
11. Demonstrate uncertainty/stale-data handling.
12. Explain future expansion.

## Positioning
Connect the working prototype to:
- precision irrigation
- farm-level weather/resource intelligence
- crop stress detection
- digital farm intelligence
- agricultural advisory

Do not claim features that are not implemented.
